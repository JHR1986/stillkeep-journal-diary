Why Stillkeep?

Your camera roll keeps photos. Stillkeep keeps the story around them.

If you’re looking for an alternative to apps like Day One, Journey or Apple Journal, Stillkeep keeps photos, words and memories together in one private journal.

Free and Premium at a glance

Stillkeep is free to download. The free plan is a complete journal with clear limits. Premium raises those limits and adds Reflect and extra Era covers. Current Premium pricing is shown in the App Store and may vary by country.

Your first ten minutes

  1. Download and sign inInstall Stillkeep from the App Store and continue with Sign in with Apple.
  2. Create your first EraName a chapter you’re living through right now, such as a trip, a new job or this year.
  3. Add three MomentsPick three photos from that chapter and write a sentence or two beside each one.
  4. Try search and Look BackSearch for a word you wrote and you’ll see how quickly a photo with a story can be found again.

Download questions

Is Stillkeep free?

Yes. Stillkeep is free to download and free to use within the starter limits of 3 Eras, 20 Moments per Era and 3 photos per Moment. Premium is optional.

Is Stillkeep available on Android or iPad?

Stillkeep is built for iPhone and requires iOS 17.6 or later.

Are there ads in the app?

No. There are no ads, no third-party advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics SDKs and no cross-app tracking.

How is Stillkeep different from Day One, Journey or Apple Journal?

Stillkeep centres on photos with their stories, organised into life chapters called Eras, with photocards and PDF photobooks for sharing. See how Stillkeep approaches journaling differently.
