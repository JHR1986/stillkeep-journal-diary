Use Stillkeep as a Writing-First Personal Diary

A diary has a different job from a photo archive: it needs to preserve what you thought and felt at the time. Stillkeep lets the written reflection stand on its own, while a photograph can be added when it helps anchor the entry to a place, person or day.

  • Separate Life Chapters: Keep work changes, relationships, personal growth, travel or everyday reflections in different Eras rather than one giant chronological notebook.
  • Writing First, Photo When Useful: Capture the reflection you want to remember and use an image as context instead of making every diary entry depend on a photograph.
  • Revisit Old Entries: Look Back brings previous Moments back by date, making the diary useful for seeing how thoughts, routines and circumstances change over time.
  • Keep a Portable Chapter: Export an Era as a formatted PDF when you want an offline copy of a period in your diary.
  • Personal by Design: The Stillkeep app uses no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking and uses Apple infrastructure including CloudKit for sync.

Free diary app questions

Can I use Stillkeep as a text-only diary?

Yes. A Moment can be writing only. Photos are optional, so a writing-first diary works just as well as a photo diary.

Can I format longer diary entries?

Yes. Rich-text stories support bold, italics and lists.

How do I keep my diary private?

Stillkeep has no social features and no ads. It syncs through your private iCloud using Apple’s CloudKit, and your iPhone’s own passcode and device protection apply.

How should I organise a diary in Stillkeep?

Use Eras for periods of your life, such as a year, a job or a relationship, rather than one long timeline. Tags and People & Places add another layer.

Can I print or back up my diary?

Export any Era as a PDF to keep an offline copy, print it or store it wherever you like. Read more about long-term journal preservation.
