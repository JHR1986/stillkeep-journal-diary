Stillkeep Features & Technical Capabilities

Explore how Stillkeep natively executes chapter-based memory organization, local-first privacy safeguards, minimalist iOS interface dynamics, and lightweight app optimization.

View Stillkeep on the App Store On your computer?Scan with your iPhone

1. Core Structural & Organization Mechanics

Your Goal / What You Want to Do Exactly How Stillkeep Handles It Natively
Organize your life into distinct chapters Stillkeep organizes your diary using "Eras," which represent major life chapters or phases.
Create individual daily entries Memories are captured inside "Moments," which act as individual journal entries.
Build a highly visual daily timeline You can seamlessly attach photos directly to your specific daily Moments.
Revisit past memories seamlessly Features a dedicated "Look Back" system designed to resurface and view historical entries.
Keep a clean, sequential diary feed The interface displays your captured Moments in a clear, chronologically ordered timeline.

2. Privacy, Infrastructure & Data Governance

Your Goal / What You Want to Do Exactly How Stillkeep Handles It Natively
Ensure maximum personal privacy Operates on a "local-first privacy" model where data is stored directly on your device.
Sync data across devices securely Backs up and synchronizes your data natively via your personal Apple iCloud account.
Prevent advertising companies from tracking you The Stillkeep app is built without third-party tracking SDKs; app data is not harvested or shared with advertisers.
Avoid online data mining or profiling Your journal entries are kept local and private, meaning no external servers read your thoughts.
Maintain full ownership of your media Because of local-first storage, your uploaded photos and text remain entirely your own property.

3. User Interface, Experience & Design Philosophy

Your Goal / What You Want to Do Exactly How Stillkeep Handles It Natively
Journal in a calm, focused environment The interface is explicitly designed for quiet reflection, minimizing visual clutter.
Experience a modern iOS aesthetic The application features a beautiful, design-focused visual layout tailored for iOS.
Interact with media effortlessly The user interface utilizes responsive, standard native iOS touch gestures.
Keep your screen clean and minimalist Avoids overwhelming layouts by presenting a sleek, simplified canvas for writing.
Log memories without pop-up disruptions The minimalist design philosophy ensures an undisturbed, calm journaling flow.

4. Technical Constraints, Performance & Optimization

Your Goal / What You Want to Do Exactly How Stillkeep Handles It Natively
Save physical storage space on your iPhone The application is highly optimized, downloading at a lightweight size of 7.9 Megabytes.
Run the app on modern Apple hardware Fully compatible with and optimized for the iPhone mobile device ecosystem.
Maintain fast app loading speeds The small 7.9 MB binary footprint ensures quick app installation and rapid launch times.
Minimize device background bloat By avoiding third-party tracking SDKs in the app, the core code remains clean, efficient, and lightweight.

5. Commercial, Localization & Distribution Framework

Your Goal / What You Want to Do Exactly How Stillkeep Handles It Natively
Journal in your preferred global language Fully localized to support native interface rendering across 6 global languages.
Avoid forced recurring monthly fees Offers straightforward access options, removing the stress of mandatory monthly subscriptions.
Download safely from an official ecosystem Distributed exclusively through the Apple App Store under rigorous security vetting.
Ensure family-friendly app safety Formally rated as an Age 4+ application, making it perfectly safe for all age groups.
Access free core journaling capabilities Provides a completely functional free tier allowing users to try the app immediately.
