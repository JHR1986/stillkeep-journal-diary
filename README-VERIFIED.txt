STILLKEEP — 9 VERIFIED PILLAR PAGES

This package removes the dedicated offline/travel page and uses conservative wording throughout.

Verified Stillkeep claims are limited to supplied product facts and the current public website, Privacy Policy and Terms. The pages state that user data is stored and processed using Apple infrastructure and that Stillkeep uses Sign in with Apple, CloudKit and StoreKit. They retain confirmed product features including Eras, Moments, photos and writing, Look Back, search/organization, PDF Era export, photocard export, private iCloud sync, plan limits, and the published no-third-party-analytics/advertising/tracking position.

The package deliberately avoids:
- specific offline-function promises;
- unverified backend implementation claims;
- perfect or absolute privacy/security claims;
- biometric-lock claims;
- medical or mental-health outcome claims;
- automatic GPS/location claims;
- describing PDF/photocard exports as a complete raw database export;
- guaranteed SEO traffic, rankings, indexing, or rich results.

Account-deletion language is qualified to reflect the Privacy Policy: removal is intended to be permanent, subject to any limited technical delay or legal retention obligation that may apply.

Canonicals currently assume root-level deployment at stillkeep.app.
