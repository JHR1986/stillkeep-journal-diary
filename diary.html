Photo diary, written diary or journal?

A diary traditionally records what happened, usually by date. A journal leans towards reflection and ideas. A photo diary sits in between: a dated record of your life, anchored by pictures, with just enough writing to keep the details. Stillkeep supports all three, because a Moment can be words, photos or both.

Effort per entryHigher: you describe everythingLower: the photo describes the scene

How to keep a photo diary without the daily pressure

  1. Pick a chapter, not a calendarStart an Era for the season of life you’re in now, such as “First year in Dublin” or “Spring 2026”, instead of committing to 365 entries.
  2. Add an entry when the day earns oneA good dinner, a hard conversation, a small win. Stillkeep has no streaks to break.
  3. Use the photo you already tookYour camera roll already holds the picture. The diary entry is the sentence it’s missing.
  4. Write the private partThe bit you wouldn’t post anywhere is usually the bit you’ll most want to reread.
  5. Let Look Back bring it backEarlier entries resurface over time, so the diary pays you back without you scrolling through years.

A photo diary entry, start to finish

Era: Moving Out · 14 March

Last night in the old flat

Photo: the empty living room, one lamp still plugged in.
Everything’s in boxes except the kettle. Sam came over with chips and we ate them on the floor because the sofa’s already gone. I didn’t expect to feel sad about a place with a broken heater, but I do. Tagged: home, Sam. Vibe: bittersweet.

Sixty words and one photo is enough to bring the whole evening back years from now.

Photo diary questions

Is Stillkeep a private diary?

Yes. There’s no social feed and no public profile. Stillkeep has no ads, no third-party analytics SDKs and no cross-app tracking, and it syncs through your private iCloud.

Do I need to write every day?

No. Stillkeep is designed for low-pressure journaling, with no streaks or daily reminders to keep up with.

Can I keep a diary without adding photos?

Yes. A Moment can be writing only. Add a picture when it helps and skip it when it doesn’t.

Can I see what I wrote on this day in past years?

Look Back resurfaces Moments from earlier periods, so older diary entries find their way back to you.
