Data Portability and Long-Term Preservation of Personal Journals

Personal journals can outlive devices, operating-system versions, and individual apps. Long-term preservation therefore benefits from a layered approach: keep the working journal usable, create portable exports of important material, and maintain independent copies according to the value of the records.

Portability is not the same as synchronization

iCloud synchronization helps keep an active journal available within Apple’s ecosystem. Portability means creating copies that can be retained outside the active application, such as Stillkeep’s manual Era PDF exports and Moment photocards.

Know what an export represents

A PDF can preserve a readable chapter and a photocard can preserve a shareable individual Moment. Neither should be marketed as a complete raw database export unless the product specifically provides that capability.

Use multiple preservation layers for irreplaceable memories

No app, device, or cloud account should be the sole repository for material that would be devastating to lose. Important exports can be copied into the user’s broader personal backup or archival system.

Plan before account deletion

Stillkeep provides in-app account deletion, and its legal materials explain that deletion is intended to remove associated account data and content. Users who want to retain material should create any desired exports before deleting.

Think in readable formats and regular reviews

Long-term preservation is easier when at least some important records exist in common formats that can be opened outside the originating app. Periodically reviewing exported PDFs and backup locations is more realistic than assuming a one-time archive will remain accessible forever.

20 practical questions about this topic

What does data portability mean for a journal?

It means being able to create useful copies of personal material outside the live app environment. Stillkeep provides PDF exports for Eras and photocard exports for individual Moments.

Is an Era PDF a raw database export?

No. It is a readable document representation of an Era, not a claim of complete database-level portability.

Why are common file formats useful?

Common formats can be opened by many tools outside the originating app, reducing dependence on one interface for reading important records.

Can Stillkeep PDFs be printed?

PDF is widely supported by printing tools and services, so an exported Era can be used as the source for a physical copy.

What is a photocard export for?

It creates a focused graphic representation of an individual Moment with an image and accompanying writing.

Should iCloud sync be my only backup plan?

For irreplaceable records, relying on any single service or device is unwise. Important exports can be included in a broader personal backup system.

What should I do before deleting my account?

Export any Eras or Moments you want to retain. Stillkeep states that account deletion cannot be undone; its Privacy Policy notes that removal may be subject to limited technical delay or legal retention obligations.

Can an exported PDF become a family archive?

It can form part of one, especially when paired with sensible file naming, multiple copies, and periodic checks that the archive remains readable.

How often should I export?

There is no universal schedule. A practical trigger is the completion of an important Era or any point when losing the current record would be especially costly.

Does long-term preservation require one perfect format?

No. Preservation is usually stronger when important information exists in more than one appropriate form or location.

What is a digital time capsule?

It is a deliberately preserved set of records intended to be revisited in the future. A completed Era plus a portable PDF copy can support that use case.

Can I preserve travel journals separately?

Yes. Each trip can be its own Era and exported independently after the journey.

Can I preserve a baby milestone journal?

Yes. A family-stage Era can gather selected milestones, photographs, and writing, then be exported as a readable chapter.

What is digital legacy planning?

It is the practical process of deciding which digital records matter, how they will be preserved, and how trusted people could access them if appropriate.

Does Stillkeep promise files will remain readable for decades?

No software can responsibly guarantee indefinite future compatibility. Using portable exports and periodically reviewing archives improves practical resilience.

Does ownership mean I never need backups?

No. Ownership and preservation are different issues; user-owned content can still be lost if all available copies disappear.

Can I share an export without sharing my whole journal?

Yes. Manual exports allow users to choose an Era or individual Moment rather than exposing the entire private journal.

How should exported files be named?

Use clear names that include the Era and, when useful, a date or year. Good naming improves future retrieval outside the app.

Is account deletion reversible?

Stillkeep’s legal materials describe deletion as permanent, so users should treat it as irreversible and preserve desired material first.

What is the simplest preservation rule?

Keep the live journal useful, export important completed chapters, and maintain independent copies of material you would not want to lose.
