About the founder

Hi, I’m John-Henry Roche

Software engineer and founder of Stillkeep: Journal & Diary, a private iOS photo journal and daily diary app, and Onuri: Notes & Life Tracker, an automatic second brain and folderless iOS note-taking app.

I build simple, private mobile products focused on capturing moments, frictionless writing, and meaningful memories through calm, secure user experiences.

Stillkeep

Stillkeep is designed for keeping photos together with the words and context that make them meaningful. Read About Stillkeep, explore the photo journal app, memory journal, or journaling and memory-keeping guides.

What I’m building

Since April 2026

Stillkeep: Journal & Diary

A private photo journal for iPhone that keeps photographs and their stories together as Moments, organised into life chapters called Eras. It has no ads and no third-party analytics, and it syncs through the user’s own iCloud.

Notes app

Onuri: Notes & Life Tracker

An iPhone notes and personal-knowledge app that connects your writing automatically, without folders to maintain.

How I make apps

Private by default

Personal data belongs to the person who wrote it. I build on Apple’s own infrastructure rather than running servers that store your journal.

Calm over compulsive

No streaks, no feeds and no engagement tricks. An app should be there when you need it and quiet when you don’t.

Built to last

Your memories should outlive any single app, which is why Stillkeep exports to PDF and photocards you keep.
