Frequently Asked Questions

What is Stillkeep photo journal app?

Stillkeep: Journal & Diary is a free private photo journal and diary app for iPhone. It keeps photos and written notes together as Moments, organized into chapters of your life called Eras, with a Look Back feature that resurfaces memories from previous years. Any Era can be exported as a formatted PDF photo book.

What makes Stillkeep different from a standard journal app?

Stillkeep suits people who want a simpler, photo-first private journal. Instead of one long timeline, it organizes memories into Eras, keeps photos and notes paired together, and exports entire chapters as PDF photo books.

Is Stillkeep a private notes app?

Yes. Stillkeep is designed around privacy with zero ads and no third-party analytics or tracking. Your journal content is stored and synced using Apple services, including iCloud.

Is Stillkeep free to download on iPhone?

Yes, Stillkeep is free to start on iOS. The free version includes 3 Eras, 20 Moments per Era, and 3 photos per Moment. A low-cost Premium tier (€3.99) expands capacity.

Is Stillkeep available on Android?

Not currently — Stillkeep is built natively for iPhone (iOS 17.6 or later).
