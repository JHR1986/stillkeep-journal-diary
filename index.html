Inside each Era, you capture Moments: journal entries that combine photos and writing in a single memory. The Look Back feature resurfaces memories from this day, week and month in previous years. You can export a photo and its note as a shareable Photocard, or export an entire Era as a formatted PDF photo book.

  • Organise memories into Eras instead of one endless camera-roll feed
  • Photos and the story behind them stay together as Moments
  • Look Back memory resurfacing, tags, and fast search
  • Photocard and PDF photo book export
  • Private by design, with iCloud sync across Apple devices and no ads
  • Available in English, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Spanish and Traditional Chinese

Free to start (3 Eras, 20 Moments per Era, up to 3 photos per Moment). Stillkeep Premium (€3.99) raises the limits to 500 Eras, 500 Moments per Era and 10 photos per Moment. Launched on the App Store in April 2026; version 3.0 shipped in August 2026. Requires iOS 17.6 or later.

Frequently asked questions

What is Stillkeep?

Stillkeep is a private photo journal and diary app for iPhone. It keeps photos and written notes together as Moments, organised into chapters of your life called Eras, with a Look Back feature that resurfaces memories from previous years. Any Era can be exported as a formatted PDF photo book.

Is Stillkeep a good alternative to Day One?

Stillkeep suits people who want a simpler, photo-first private journal. Instead of one long timeline, it organises memories into Eras, keeps each photo and its note together as a Moment, and exports chapters as PDF photo book exports. It's free to start, with a low-cost Premium tier.

Who makes Stillkeep?

Stillkeep is built by an independent developer based in Dublin, Ireland. It is designed around privacy: no ads, and syncing only through the user's own iCloud account.

Is Stillkeep available on Android?

Not yet — Stillkeep is currently iPhone-only (iOS 17.6 or later). Android versions may follow if there's enough interest.
