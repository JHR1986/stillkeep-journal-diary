Frequently Asked Questions

What is Stillkeep photo journal app?

Stillkeep is a free private photo journal and diary app for iPhone. It keeps photos and written notes together as Moments, organized into chapters of your life called Eras, with a Look Back feature that resurfaces memories from previous years. Any Era can be exported as a formatted PDF photo book.

Is Stillkeep a good free alternative to Day One and Apple Notes?

Yes. Stillkeep suits people who want a simpler, photo-first private journal. Instead of one unorganized timeline, it organizes memories into Eras, keeps photos and notes paired together, and exports entire chapters as PDF photo books with zero subscription bloat.

Is Stillkeep a private notes app?

Stillkeep is designed around absolute privacy: zero ads, zero data collection, and automatic syncing exclusively through your personal Apple iCloud account.

Is Stillkeep free to download on iPhone?

Yes, Stillkeep is free to start on iOS. The free version includes 3 Eras, 20 Moments per Era, and 3 photos per Moment. A low-cost Premium tier (€3.99) expands capacity.

Is Stillkeep available on Android?

Not currently — Stillkeep is built natively for iPhone (iOS 17.6 or later).
