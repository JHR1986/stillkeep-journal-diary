Wedding memory book

Make a wedding memory book that tells the whole story.

Your wedding was more than a gallery.

Professional photographs capture how it looked. The notes you write afterwards preserve what it felt like — the private jokes, the unplanned detours, the words you almost forgot.

Start your journal on iPhone ↗

Private photo memories, in chapters called Eras.

Remember the moments between the photographs.

A wedding day moves quickly. Make room for the less photogenic details, from the first coffee to the conversation in the car afterwards. They often become the stories you tell for years.

  • The morning: who was with you, what was playing, what made you laugh.
  • The ceremony: one line from your vows or a moment you did not expect.
  • The reception: the speech that stayed with you and the song that brought everyone up.

Make a keepsake that keeps growing.

A wedding memory book does not need to end with the last dance. Start with the engagement, add the wedding day, and let a new chapter follow for married life.

  • Pair candid photographs with a few honest sentences.
  • Include the story behind a place, a guest or a meaningful object.
  • Use a separate Era for your honeymoon if that trip becomes its own story.

Inside Stillkeep

Organise the chapter, not just the date.

Stillkeep’s Eras give different periods of life their own home. You might create “Our Wedding”, then another Era for a honeymoon or your first year together. The example shows how each chapter can be revisited without scrolling through a camera roll.
