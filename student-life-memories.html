STUDENT LIFE

Keep the moments that make student life yours.

The big milestones matter. So do late-night chats, new friendships and that tiny café you visited between classes.

Get Stillkeep free on iPhone ↗

Build your student story

  • First week: what felt new or unfamiliar?
  • People: who made an ordinary day better?
  • Places: where did you spend time between classes?
  • Small wins: what felt difficult until it didn’t?
  • Milestones: exams, projects, moving day or graduation.

A quick entry to try

  • Choose one photo from this week.
  • Name one person, place or feeling in it.
  • Write what you would want to tell yourself five years from now.

Try this: Example: “We studied until the library closed, bought chips on the way home and laughed about how little we remembered.”
