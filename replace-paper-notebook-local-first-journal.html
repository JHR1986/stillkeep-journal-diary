Replacing Paper Notebooks with a Local-First Digital Journal

Moving from a paper notebook to an iPhone journal does not mean you have to change the way you reflect. You can keep the simple habit of writing only when you want to, while gaining useful digital features such as photos, search, organized life chapters, and exports.

If you are deciding between paper and digital journaling, start with our complete guide to journal apps, digital diaries and journaling on iPhone.

Start with your life now, not every old notebook

You do not need to type years of old notebooks into Stillkeep before it becomes useful. Start with one current Era, add new Moments from now on, and see whether combining photos and writing suits the way you like to remember things.

Keep the freedom to write only when you want to

One of the nice things about a paper journal is that you choose when to open it. Stillkeep can work the same way: add a Moment when something feels worth remembering, without worrying about missed days or maintaining a streak.

Let digital features make journaling easier, not busier

You can use search, tags, vibes, pins, photos, and Look Back when they are useful, but you do not need to use everything. A Moment can stay as simple as a date, a few words, and a photo.

Give each part of your life a simple name

Stillkeep organizes memories into Eras rather than asking you to build a complicated folder system. Names such as “First Year in Dublin,” “Garden Project,” or “Japan 2026” can be enough to make a chapter easy to recognize later.

Turn important digital chapters into something you can keep

A paper notebook already exists as a physical object, while a digital journal may benefit from an extra copy. Stillkeep can export an important or completed Era as a PDF that you can keep, share, or print using other tools.

20 common questions about moving from a paper journal to an iPhone journal

What is the easiest way to move from paper to Stillkeep?

Start with one part of your life that matters now and create an Era for it. You can record new Moments from this point forward without digitizing all your old notebooks first.

Do I need to journal every day after switching?

No. Stillkeep does not require daily entries or streaks, so you can write as often or as occasionally as you did with a paper notebook.

What does digital journaling add to paper?

Digital journaling makes it possible to search your writing, add photos, use tags, vibes and pins, rediscover memories with Look Back, and create exports. Stillkeep keeps those tools inside a private personal-journal experience.

Can I keep old paper journals too?

Yes. There is no need to replace or throw away old notebooks. You can keep your paper journals and use Stillkeep for new memories or selected parts of your life.

How should I name my first Era?

Choose a name that describes a part of your life you will recognize later, such as a trip, new job, family stage, creative project, or a particular year.

Can a Moment be mostly writing?

Yes. Stillkeep has separate options for adding writing and photos, so you can create a written Moment even when you do not have or need a picture.

Can a Moment be mostly photographic?

Yes. You can add multiple photos to a Moment and keep the writing short if the pictures already tell most of the story.

How do I avoid turning digital journaling into admin?

Keep it simple. Use tags, vibes, and pins only when they genuinely help you organize or find a memory later.

Can I search my writing later?

Yes. Stillkeep can search your journal entries, tags, and Era titles, making old writing easier to find than manually turning through pages.

What is useful about Look Back for former paper journalers?

Look Back brings older Moments back from different points in the past. It gives you a way to rediscover an entry without searching through old pages or remembering exactly where it was.

Can I print a digital journal chapter?

Yes. You can export an Era as a PDF and then print it using compatible printing tools or services.

Does Stillkeep have public social features?

No. Stillkeep is designed as a private personal journal rather than a public social feed.

Can I share just one memory?

Yes. You can export one Moment as a photocard containing its image and accompanying writing.

What if I prefer short entries?

That is completely fine. A Moment can be short and only needs enough writing to help you remember what mattered.

How can photos improve a notebook-style habit?

A photo can preserve faces, places, objects, and small visual details, while your writing can explain why the moment mattered or how it felt.

Is Stillkeep folder-based?

Not primarily. Stillkeep organizes Moments inside life chapters called Eras, with tags and other tools available when you want extra organization.

What if I miss handwriting?

Digital journaling will not reproduce the physical feeling of pen and paper. You can keep using paper when you want that experience and use Stillkeep where photos, search, synchronization, and exports are more useful.

Can I create a digital family archive?

Yes. You can create Eras for family stages and milestones and later export important chapters. For irreplaceable family memories, keeping additional copies outside any single app is sensible.

How much can I try for free?

The free version supports up to 3 Eras, 20 Moments in each Era, and 3 photos in each Moment, giving you room to try the main journaling approach before subscribing.

When would Premium become useful?

Premium is designed for people who keep using Stillkeep and need much more space. It supports up to 500 Eras, 500 Moments per Era, and 10 photos per Moment.
