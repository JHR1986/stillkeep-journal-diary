What Is Included in the Free Version?

This page is specifically about the free Stillkeep plan. You can start a real journal without entering a large archive first or guessing which basics are paywalled. The starter tier is intentionally bounded: up to 3 Eras and 20 Moments inside each Era.

  • Up to 60 Moments: Use three separate Eras with as many as 20 photo-and-note Moments in each — enough to test travel, family, daily reflection or another journaling style.
  • Photos & Notes Together: A Moment keeps the image and its written context in one place instead of splitting the memory between Photos and a notes app.
  • Look Back: Resurface older Moments by date so the free journal is useful for revisiting memories, not only recording them.
  • PDF Export: Export an Era as a formatted PDF when you want a portable copy outside the app.
  • Private Apple Sync: In the Stillkeep app, there are no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking; Stillkeep uses Apple infrastructure including CloudKit for its sync architecture.

Free Should Be Easy to Understand

The starter plan is not described as unlimited. Its boundaries are shown up front, so you can decide whether 3 Eras and 20 Moments per Era fit the way you want to journal before upgrading.
