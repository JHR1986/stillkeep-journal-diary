Travel photo journal

A travel journal app for the stories behind your photos.

Bring home the story, not just the photos.

You remember the view. But do you remember the conversation on the train, the wrong turn that became your favourite café, or why everyone laughed at dinner?

Start your journal on iPhone ↗

Private photo memories, in chapters called Eras.

Write down what a camera roll cannot.

A travel journal is most useful when it preserves context. Instead of narrating every hour, choose a few moments each day that you would struggle to explain from pictures alone.

  • Save the name of the tiny restaurant you stumbled into.
  • Write what surprised you about a place, not just where you went.
  • Keep the soundtrack of a journey: a song, a phrase or a shared joke.

A simple starting point

Try this in your own journal

01
Name the trip

Make an Era for a holiday, weekend away or long journey.

02
Capture one moment a day

Choose a single image and add two sentences: what happened, and why it mattered.

03
Keep the imperfect bits

Delayed trains, rainy days and wrong turns often become the best stories.

Let the journey have a beginning, middle and end.

Travel photos pile up quickly. A little structure turns hundreds of images into a story you can revisit: anticipation, arrival, discovery and the unexpected moments in between.

  • Use place and trip tags so memories can be found again.
  • Include a departure-day note, not just highlights from the destination.
  • Finish with one thing you would return for — or do differently.
