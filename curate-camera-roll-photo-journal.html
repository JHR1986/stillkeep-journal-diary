Curation Over Collection: Turning Camera Roll Photos into Memories

A camera roll is excellent at collecting images, but collection and memory keeping are different jobs. Stillkeep’s photo-journal workflow is built around choosing meaningful images and attaching the context that makes them worth returning to.

View Stillkeep on the App Store

Select for meaning, not completeness

A Moment does not need every photograph from an event. Choosing a smaller set of representative images can make the memory easier to revisit while leaving the full photographic archive in the user’s photo library.

Add the information a photograph cannot show

Names, reasons, feelings, jokes, decisions, and small details often disappear faster than the image itself. Written context beside the photograph can preserve those details without requiring a long diary entry.

Use visual inspection deliberately

Stillkeep’s full-screen photo viewer and pinch-to-zoom support let users revisit details within saved photographs. That is especially useful for images where small visual elements carry part of the memory.

Create thematic visual chapters

Travel, a new home, a creative project, a baby’s milestones, or a season can each become an Era. The result is closer to a curated visual story than a chronological dump of every image taken.

Export the finished story

An Era can be exported to PDF as a digital photobook-style record, while an individual Moment can become a photocard. These exports give users different ways to preserve or deliberately share curated material.

20 practical questions about this topic

What is photo curation?

Photo curation means selecting images because they contribute to a meaningful record rather than preserving every image in the same journal context.

Does Stillkeep replace the iPhone photo library?

It is better understood as a journal for selected memories, not as a claim to replace every function of a general photo library.

How many photos can a Moment contain?

The free plan supports up to 3 photos per Moment; Premium supports up to 10.

Why add writing to a photo?

Writing can preserve people’s names, context, feelings, reasons, and small details that may not be visible in the image.

Can I inspect details in a saved photo?

Yes. Stillkeep includes full-screen viewing with pinch-to-zoom.

How can an Era help curate travel photos?

A travel Era groups selected trip photographs and writing into one bounded story, separating them from unrelated memories.

Should I copy every photo from an event into Stillkeep?

Not necessarily. Selective curation can make the journal easier to revisit while the complete photo collection remains in the user’s normal photo library.

Can tags connect visually related Moments?

Yes. Tags can provide a thematic layer across Moments and can be searched later.

What are vibes useful for in a photo journal?

Vibes offer a lightweight way to add another descriptor to a Moment alongside its photos, text, Era, and tags.

Can important photo Moments be pinned?

Yes. Pins can mark selected Moments as especially notable.

How does search help with a visual archive?

Stillkeep can search text, tags, and Era titles and offers filters including photos, helping users narrow a growing journal.

What does Look Back add to photo curation?

It resurfaces earlier Moments from different points in the past, allowing curated photographs and their context to be encountered again.

Can I create a digital photobook?

Stillkeep can export an entire Era as a PDF, producing a portable photobook-style record.

Can I make a shareable card from one photo Moment?

Yes. Stillkeep can export an individual Moment as a photocard containing the image and accompanying writing.

Does Stillkeep preserve every original camera-roll function?

No. A curated journal and a full photo-management system have different purposes. Stillkeep focuses on meaningful selected memories.

How should I choose photos for a Moment?

Choose images that reveal different useful aspects of the memory rather than near-duplicates. The aim is context and recall, not maximum image count.

Can I create an Era around a visual project?

Yes. A renovation, garden, artwork, collection, or other visual progression can be documented as an Era.

Is a photo enough without text?

Sometimes. Stillkeep allows photo-oriented Moments, but even a short note can make an image more understandable years later.

Does photo access require permission?

Yes. Stillkeep’s privacy policy describes photo-library access as permission-based for adding images selected by the user.

What is the core curation principle?

Keep the images that help tell the story, add the context future-you may forget, and organize them in a chapter that will still make sense later.
