Why your photos lose their story

A photo records what something looked like. It rarely records who took it, what had just happened, the joke everyone was laughing at or why you stopped to take it. Those details fade within months, and a camera roll gives them nowhere to live. Adding a few lines beside the photos that matter is the simplest way to keep them.

How to add a story to a photo on iPhone

  1. Create a MomentTap to add a new Moment and pick one or more photos. The free plan allows up to 3 photos per Moment; Premium allows up to 10.
  2. Write what the photo can’t showAdd as much or as little as you like. Rich-text stories support bold, italics and lists for longer memories.
  3. Add People & PlacesRecord who was there and where it happened, so you can filter and search by them later.
  4. Tag it and choose a vibeTags and vibes add a second way to find the Moment again, beyond the date it was taken.
  5. Save it to an EraPlace the Moment in a life chapter, such as a trip, a first year in a new city or a child’s early years.

What to write beside a photo

You don’t need a paragraph. One or two specific sentences will do more for your future self than a long caption written out of duty. Try answering one of these:

What happened just before?

“We’d been lost for an hour and this was the first café that was open.”

What can’t the camera see?

The smell of the bakery, the song in the car, how cold the water actually was.

Who is behind the camera?

The person who took the picture is usually missing from it. Name them.

Why did you keep this one?

Out of thirty near-identical shots, say why this is the one that matters.

Questions about adding notes to photos

Can I add notes to photos on iPhone without changing the original?

Yes. In Stillkeep the note lives in a Moment alongside the photo. Your original picture stays in your Photos library unchanged.

How many photos can one note or Moment hold?

Up to 3 photos per Moment on the free plan and up to 10 on Stillkeep Premium, so a single story can cover a whole afternoon or event.

Can I search my photo notes later?

Yes. Stillkeep searches the writing in your Moments, and you can also filter by tags, vibes, People & Places and Eras.

Are my photo notes private?

Stillkeep has no ads, no third-party analytics SDKs and no cross-app tracking. Sync uses your private iCloud through Apple’s CloudKit.

Can I share a photo together with its story?

Yes. Export any Moment as a memory photocard, or export a whole Era as a PDF photobook. You choose exactly what leaves the app.
