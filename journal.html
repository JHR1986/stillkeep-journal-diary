What is a visual journal?

A visual journal is a journal where pictures do part of the remembering. Instead of a page of handwriting, each entry pairs an image with a few lines of writing: the picture holds the scene and the words hold the meaning. It suits people who think in images, people who find blank pages intimidating, and anyone whose phone is already full of photos that deserve a sentence or two.

Stillkeep is built for this format. Every entry is a Moment with photos and writing together, and Moments are gathered into Eras, the chapters of your life.

Four visual journal formats that actually last

Low effort

One picture a week

Choose the photo that best sums up your week and write two sentences about it. Fifty-two entries make a remarkably complete year.

Event-based

Only when something happens

Trips, birthdays, firsts, endings. No schedule, just a Moment whenever life gives you one worth keeping.

Project

A single subject over time

A renovation, a training plan, a pregnancy or a garden, kept as one Era from beginning to end.

Reflective

Image as a prompt

Pick any photo, even an old one, and use it as a starting point to write about what was happening in your life at the time.

Visual journal questions

Is a visual journal the same as a photo journal?

They overlap. A photo journal is a visual journal that uses photographs specifically. See photo journal examples for sample entries.

Do I have to write a lot?

No. One or two specific sentences per picture are enough. Stillkeep doesn’t use streaks or daily targets, so you can journal only when something feels worth keeping.

Can I keep a visual journal without photos?

Yes. A Moment can hold writing on its own, so text-only entries can sit beside picture entries in the same Era.

How do I organise a visual journal?

Group entries into Eras: a year, a trip or a project. Read more about chapter-based journaling.

Can I export my visual journal?

Yes. Each Era can be exported as a formatted PDF photobook.
