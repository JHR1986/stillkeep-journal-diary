The idea behind photocards

Inspired by the joy of collecting.

I’ve always been interested in how people collect and share K-pop photocards, Pokémon trading cards and sports cards. Each card gives one image its own identity, combining it with colour, text and small details that make it feel worth keeping.

That sparked an idea: what if you could do something similar with your own memories?

Stillkeep lets you turn any Moment into a digital photocard. Your photograph is presented alongside its story, emojis and memory details, surrounded by colours drawn from its Era. The result is a personal, shareable keepsake rather than another photo disappearing into a camera roll.

One Moment. One card. One story worth keeping.

How it works

From private Moment to shareable card.

No templates to wrestle with and no complicated design tools. Your Moment already contains everything the card needs.

1

Choose the photograph and story you want to send to a friend or family member.

2

Export Photocard

Stillkeep brings together the image, words, emojis, date, tags and colours from its Era.

3

Share it your way

Save the finished photocard or send it using whichever messaging or sharing app you prefer.

🔒

Your journal stays private.

You decide which individual Moments leave Stillkeep and who receives them. Sharing a photocard does not make the rest of your journal public.

Photocard questions

What is a memory photocard?

A digital card made from one Stillkeep Moment. It shows the photo with its story, emojis, date and tags, framed in colours drawn from the Moment’s Era.

Is this a trading-card game or a printing service?

No. Photocards are personal digital keepsakes inspired by collectible cards. They are images you can save, send or print yourself.

Does sharing a photocard make my journal public?

No. Only the Moment you export leaves the app, and only with the people you send it to.

Which apps can I share a photocard with?

Any app in the iPhone share sheet, including Messages, Mail and WhatsApp, or you can simply save it to Photos.

Share more than a photograph.

Send the picture with the words, feeling and little details that made it worth remembering in the first place.
