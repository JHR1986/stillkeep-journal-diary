Digital Minimalism and Low-Pressure Memory Keeping

Digital minimalism does not mean giving up useful technology. For journaling, it can simply mean keeping the memories that matter, organizing them without unnecessary pressure, and returning to them when you want to. Stillkeep is designed around that idea with private Moments organized into meaningful Eras.

View Stillkeep on the App Store

Journal when you actually have something to remember

Stillkeep does not require daily entries or streaks. You can add a Moment after something meaningful happens, during a trip, at the end of a project, or simply whenever you feel like recording something.

A journal entry does not need to be long

Sometimes one photo and a few words are enough to preserve a memory. You can write more when you want to, but every Moment does not need to become a long diary entry.

Make memories easy to find without over-organizing

Eras keep related Moments together, while tags, vibes, pins, search, and filters can help when you need them. You do not have to carefully label everything; the aim is simply to make important memories easier to find later.

Rediscover memories without having to search for them

Look Back brings previous Moments back from different points in the past. It gives you a way to rediscover things you saved without having to search through your journal or constantly add something new.

A calmer approach, without making health claims

Some people simply prefer journaling without streaks, daily targets, or pressure to keep up. Stillkeep is a journaling and memory-keeping app, not a treatment for anxiety, depression, stress, or any other health condition.

20 common questions about low-pressure digital journaling

Does Stillkeep require a daily journaling streak?

No. You can record the Moments you want to remember without having to make an entry every day.

What does low-pressure journaling mean?

Low-pressure journaling means deciding for yourself when to write and how much to record. Missing a day, a week, or longer does not mean you have failed at keeping a journal.

Can I write only when something important happens?

Yes. Stillkeep can be used only when something happens that you want to remember, rather than requiring a daily routine.

Does Stillkeep claim to treat anxiety or stress?

No. It is a journaling and memory-keeping app, not a medical treatment or mental-health intervention.

Why use Eras in a minimalist journal?

Eras let you organize memories into broad chapters such as a trip, university, a new baby, or a project. A few meaningful chapters can be enough without creating a complicated filing system.

Do I need to tag every Moment?

No. Tags are there when they help you organize or find something later, but you do not need to add them to every Moment.

What are vibes for?

Vibes are a simple way to add another description to a Moment. You can use them when they are helpful and leave them out when they are not.

Can I keep entries short?

Yes. A Moment can be as simple as a short piece of writing or a photo with a little context; it does not have to be a long reflection.

Can photos replace some writing?

Yes. A photo can capture much of the memory visually, while a few words can preserve details or feelings that the picture cannot show.

How can Look Back support reflection?

Look Back brings older Moments back from different points in the past, so you can rediscover memories without searching for them yourself.

Does Stillkeep have a public social feed?

No. It is designed as a private personal journal.

Can I share something intentionally?

Yes. You can choose to export a Moment as a photocard or a whole Era as a PDF, then decide whether you want to keep or share the exported file.

How can I avoid over-organizing?

Start with the Era and the Moment itself. Add tags, vibes, or pins only when they genuinely make the journal easier for you to organize or revisit.

Is digital minimalism the same as using no technology?

No. Digital minimalism can simply mean using technology deliberately instead of feeling that you have to use every feature or constantly interact with an app.

Can Stillkeep be used weekly instead of daily?

Yes. Stillkeep does not require you to journal on a fixed schedule, so weekly, occasional, or more frequent use can all fit.

What is a sustainable journaling routine?

The best routine is one that feels easy enough to continue. That might mean frequent short Moments for one person and occasional detailed entries for someone else.

Does Stillkeep send advertising notifications?

Stillkeep’s Privacy Policy states that third-party advertising services are not used. This guide does not make additional claims about notification behavior beyond the app’s documented features and settings.

Can a finished Era help create closure?

Some people may like having a clear end to a trip, project, or stage of life and keeping it as a finished Era. Whether that creates a feeling of closure is personal, so Stillkeep does not promise a psychological outcome.

Who might prefer low-pressure journaling?

It may suit people who want to keep meaningful memories but do not want journaling to become a daily obligation.

What is the main principle?

Keep what matters to you, organize only as much as you find useful, and come back to your journal on your own schedule.
