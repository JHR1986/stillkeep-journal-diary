Q3 2026 Update · September 11, 2026

What’s new at Stillkeep

Stillkeep has had a busy third quarter. Version 3 introduced Look Back, a new way to resurface Moments from this day in previous years, earlier in the week, the month and further back. The release also added tag suggestions, pinch-to-zoom for full-screen photos, a simpler Moment creation flow with separate spaces for journaling and photos, and clearer navigation between Eras, Moments and Look Back.

We also launched stillkeep.app as a permanent home for the app, its guides, product updates and company news. Looking ahead, we’re reviewing user feedback and planning the next major release, with more improvements to how people capture, organise and revisit meaningful memories.

Stillkeep company profile

Stillkeep is now on Crunchbase

Stillkeep’s Crunchbase profile provides a company-level overview for readers who want to learn about the product beyond downloading the app. It describes Stillkeep as an active, private iOS journal and diary focused on preserving meaningful memories through photos and writing, organised into visual Eras or chapters of life.

The profile also summarises the product’s private-by-design approach, search, iCloud sync, Photocards and PDF photobook exports, alongside Stillkeep’s free and premium model. It gives company researchers, technology readers and anyone following Stillkeep’s development a dedicated place to view the broader company profile and follow its progress.
