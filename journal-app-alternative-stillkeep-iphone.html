Journal App Alternative for iPhone: How Stillkeep Approaches Journaling Differently

If you are searching for an alternative journal app on iPhone, Stillkeep offers a distinct way to organize a private journal. This guide focuses on Stillkeep’s confirmed features and how its workflow differs from common approaches to digital journaling, without suggesting that one style is right for everyone.

Stillkeep in one sentence

Stillkeep is a private photo-and-writing journal for iPhone that organizes life into Eras, with dated Moments inside each Era, and does not require a daily journaling streak.

Stillkeep offers a different kind of journaling workflow

Journal apps can be designed around many different workflows, including continuous timelines, daily writing habits, prompts, folders, or broad multimedia collections. Stillkeep takes a focused iPhone approach built around meaningful life chapters rather than trying to make every journaling style work the same way.

Stillkeep takes a more focused iPhone approach. Its main organizing idea is the Era: a chapter such as University, New Baby, Travelling 2026, a hobby, or a personal project. Dated Moments sit inside those chapters and can contain photos and writing.

Why someone might look for a different journal app

People search for alternatives for many reasons: they may want a different way to organize memories, a different writing rhythm, a different subscription structure, or simply a journaling experience that feels more natural to them.

Stillkeep’s clearest difference is its Era-based structure. Instead of treating the journal only as an ongoing stream, it lets you create distinct chapters of life and keep related Moments inside them.

An alternative journal approach for people who think in life chapters

Some people naturally remember life as periods: university, a relationship, a new home, travelling, a hobby, or a project. Stillkeep is built directly around that idea.

Each Era can contain dated Moments, so you still keep chronology while also preserving the larger context of where that memory belongs.

A journal without a required daily streak

Some journaling approaches are built around regular writing, prompts, reminders, or streaks, which can suit people who enjoy building a consistent habit.

Stillkeep takes a lower-pressure approach: there is no requirement to write every day or maintain a streak. You can add a Moment when something feels worth keeping and leave as much time as you like between entries.

Photo journaling and memory keeping

Stillkeep centers visual journaling on combining selected photos with written context inside Moments. Full-screen viewing and pinch-to-zoom make it easy to revisit visual details later.

Stillkeep can also export an individual Moment as a photocard, while an entire Era can be exported as a PDF digital photobook-style record.

Stillkeep’s privacy approach

Stillkeep’s published Privacy Policy says user data is stored and processed using Apple infrastructure and identifies CloudKit, Sign in with Apple, and StoreKit among the Apple services it uses. Stillkeep free vs. Premium

The free Stillkeep version supports up to 3 Eras, 20 Moments per Era, and 3 photos per Moment. That is enough to try the Era-and-Moment workflow before paying.

Stillkeep Premium is €3.99 per month based on the current product information supplied for this page and raises capacity to up to 500 Eras, 500 Moments per Era, and 10 photos per Moment.
