Less camera roll. More meaning.

A useful gallery is not every screenshot, duplicate and accidental photo you have ever taken. Stillkeep is for the pictures you deliberately keep — the ones with a person, place, feeling or story attached.

Browse life as Moments and Eras.

Your Moments form a visual stream, while Eras gather them into chapters. Open a photo to return to its caption, date, tags and context, or search when you remember the story but not where the picture is.

A complete library versus a curated gallery

Your Photos app is a complete library: everything you have ever captured, in the order you captured it. A curated gallery is the opposite. It holds only the images you chose, in the groupings that make sense to you, with enough context to understand them years later.

How the Stillkeep gallery is organised

Moments feed

Filters

Gallery ideas worth starting

A small, focused gallery is easier to keep going than a perfect archive. A few that work well as Eras:

Best of the year

Twelve to thirty photos, one or two a month, each with a line about why it made the cut.

A home, before and after

Rooms, renovations and the small moments that turned a house into a home.

A child’s first years

Firsts, funny phrases and ordinary days, written down while you still remember them.

A garden through the seasons

The same corner photographed over months, with notes on what grew and what didn’t.

A pet’s life

Their habits, their favourite places and the stories everyone in the family retells.

One city, many visits

A single place you keep returning to, with each visit recorded as its own Moment.

Private photo gallery questions

Does Stillkeep replace the Photos app?

No. Photos remains your complete library. Stillkeep is a separate, private gallery for the photos you want to keep with their stories.

Is the gallery private?

Yes. Stillkeep has no social feed, no ads and no third-party analytics SDKs. Your gallery syncs through your private iCloud using Apple’s CloudKit.

Can I view photos full screen?

Yes. Any photo in a Moment opens in a full-screen viewer with pinch-to-zoom.

Can I turn a gallery into something I can print or share?

Yes. Export an Era as a PDF photobook to save, print or email, or share a single Moment as a photocard.

How large can a gallery get?

The free plan includes 3 Eras with up to 20 Moments each. Premium raises this to 500 Eras with up to 500 Moments each and 10 photos per Moment.
