# Stillkeep: Private iOS Photo Journal & Daily Diary App

Stillkeep is a private, ad-free iOS journaling app designed to capture life's moments and organize them into meaningful life eras. This repository serves as the official landing page hub and SEO infrastructure for Stillkeep.

## 📱 About Stillkeep
Stillkeep is more than just a note-taking app—it is a private, secure digital space for reflection and memory keeping. By leveraging Apple's on-device intelligence and private iCloud sync, Stillkeep automatically organizes your photos and text entries, allowing you to browse your life's journey by chronological eras.

### Why Choose Stillkeep?
* **Private by Design:** Your journals remain yours. We use native iCloud sync, ensuring no third-party servers or advertisers ever see your personal memories.
* **Chronological Memory Organization:** Forget complex file structures or endless scrolling. Stillkeep automatically groups your entries into meaningful eras, making it effortless to look back on your personal history.
* **Ad-Free Experience:** Zero trackers, zero ads, zero distractions. Just you and your memories.
* **Built Exclusively for iOS:** Designed natively for the iPhone experience with high performance, low friction, and accessibility in mind.
