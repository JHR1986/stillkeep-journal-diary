## 📱 About Stillkeep: Journal & Diary
**Stillkeep: Journal & Diary** is a secure private journal and diary app built for capturing photos, writing, and the moments that matter, beautifully organised into **Eras**—chapters of your life you'll revisit for years to come. Whether it's a trip abroad, your baby's first year, a wedding, a concert, a personal project, or everyday life, create an Era for every chapter and keep your photos, words, and memories together in beautiful **Moments**.

### Core Features of Stillkeep: Journal & Diary
* **Private by design:** Your **Stillkeep: Journal & Diary** app data belongs strictly to you with seamless iCloud sync across your Apple devices.
* **Rich Capturing:** Capture Moments with high-resolution photos, personal writing, or both.
* **Eras & Moments:** Organise your life chapters into beautiful Eras and rediscover memories with **Look Back**.
* **Powerful Tools:** Find any memory instantly with powerful search, share beautiful Photocards, or export complete Era PDFs as digital photobooks.
* **Distraction-Free:** Clean, calm, and private with a zero-ad experience.

### Free vs. Premium Tiers
* **Free:** Up to 3 Eras, up to 20 Moments per Era, and up to 180 photos across your journal (up to 3 per Moment).
* **Premium:** Up to 500 Eras, up to 500 Moments per Era, and up to 10 Photos per Moment.
