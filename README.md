**Stillkeep: Journal & Diary** is a private, local-first photo journal and diary for iPhone. It keeps photographs and the words behind them together as **Moments**, grouped into chapters of life called **Eras**.

Instead of leaving important memories scattered through an endless camera roll, Stillkeep gives the photos that matter a place to keep their story — a trip, a baby's first year, a wedding, a new home, a project, or everyday life.

## How it works

* **Moments** — a photo (or several) with the writing that explains why it mattered.
* **Eras** — chapters that group related Moments around a period, person, place, project or experience.
* **Look Back** — resurfaces memories from earlier periods.
* **Search** — find a Moment again by what you remember.

That makes Stillkeep work as a photo journal, private diary, memory journal, travel or family journal, gratitude journal, digital scrapbook or personal memory archive.

## Key features

* Photos and writing together in each Moment
* Eras for organising memories into life chapters
* Look Back and fast search
* Tags, Vibes and pins
* Photocard export for sharing a single Moment
* Era PDF export for keeping a whole chapter as a photobook
* iCloud / CloudKit sync with Sign in with Apple
* No ads, no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, no cross-app tracking

## Free & Premium

* **Free:** up to 3 Eras, 20 Moments per Era and 3 photos per Moment.
* **Premium:** up to 500 Eras, 500 Moments per Era and 10 photos per Moment.

Current pricing is shown in the App Store and may vary by storefront.

## Private by design

Stillkeep is built for personal memories, not advertising. It uses Apple services — Sign in with Apple, CloudKit and StoreKit — for account, sync and purchases, and does not use third-party advertising, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking. See the [Privacy Policy](https://jhr1986.github.io/stillkeep-legal/privacy.html) and [Terms of Use](https://jhr1986.github.io/stillkeep-legal/terms.html).
